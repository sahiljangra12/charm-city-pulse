Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Charm City Pulse is a free public-information app about Baltimore's environment. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no server of its own. It is a set of static files, and we never receive or store your data.

What stays on your device

Your browser keeps a few preferences: light or dark theme, °F or °C, resident or researcher mode, and your last searched location so distances can be shown. They stay in your browser's local storage and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Location

If you tap "My location", your browser asks for permission. Your coordinates are used only on your device to find your census tract and nearby centers. They are sent to the U.S. government's FEMA flood map service only to look up the flood zone at that point.

Third-party services

To show live information, your browser requests data directly from these public services. They receive your IP address and the request (for example, an address you type) under their own privacy policies:

  • Address search: Esri ArcGIS World Geocoder, Photon (Komoot) and OpenStreetMap Nominatim
  • Weather and air: Open-Meteo, the National Weather Service and EPA AirNow (via Esri ArcGIS Online)
  • Water and flooding: USGS Water Data APIs and the FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer
  • Maps: Esri basemaps, OpenStreetMap and the Overpass API
  • Code libraries and fonts: cdnjs, unpkg and Google Fonts

Children

The app is general-audience public information and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
